Themes the trials agree on

Money management: spending wisely, saving, debt and bankruptcy

In 13 of 17 papers and every trial of 2026, from Form 4 Unit 10 (Pengurusan Perbelanjaan) and Form 5 Unit 10 (Pengurusan Kewangan). The angles repeat: an overspending character (Aminah, Alvin, Puan Maria's credit cards), "20,000 muflis a year", saving through SSPN or a savings habit, and agencies such as AKPK. It was a 20-mark Section C question in 4 papers. Know causes of overspending, effects of debt, ways to manage money, and the role of SSPN and AKPK.

Information ethics: fake news, verifying sources, online scams

13 of 17 papers, mostly as Section A or B. Fake news is the single most set issue after national icons (9 papers): why people share, effects on unity, how to check (sebenarnya.my, official portals) and sources that can be trusted. Online scams are rising (2 papers in 2025, 2 in 2026, with PDRM statistics). Kelantan 2026 Q1 reuses Terengganu 2025 Q5 word for word.

National security and sovereignty: borders, PATI, Rukun Negara, the Constitutional Monarchy

6 of 7 trials of 2026 (2025: 5 of 10), the clearest rise between the two years. Border control and smuggling, illegal immigrants and refugees, the role of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, and what citizens can do to defend sovereignty. Kelantan and Selangor 1 made it a 20-mark Section C question in 2026.

Integrity, now personal: exam cheating, plagiarism, honest traders, tax

12 of 17 papers. In 2025 integrity meant corruption and organisations (the anti-corruption plan, EAIC, bribery). Similarities between papers
  • Copied stimulus. Kelantan 2026 Q1 uses the same social-media monologue as Terengganu 2025 Q5 ("Oh, ramai yang berkongsi tentang mesej ini!"), with different parts.
  • States echo their own previous paper. Sarawak 2026 repeats drug mules, online scams and overspending from Sarawak 2025. Melaka repeats a pupil late for school because of gaming (Argon in 2025, Farid in 2026), community programmes, fake news and unity. Kelantan repeats fake news, glokal individuals, the UN and community programmes. Pahang repeats saving, Malaysian students abroad and ASEAN.
  • Shared news hooks. "20,000 declared bankrupt every year" (Labuan and Sarawak 2025), Malaysia as ASEAN chair (Melaka 2025, Pahang 2026), illegal immigrant deportations (Sarawak and Selangor 1 2026), LHDN tax relief (Pahang and Perlis 2026), drug mules at foreign airports (Sarawak 2025 and 2026), refugees on boats (Perlis 2025, Kelantan 2026).
  • Same dilemma shapes. A character torn between duty and loyalty: a prefect who catches a sibling skipping school, a student who sees a friend cheat, a bullying victim deciding whether to report, a son choosing between a job abroad and his parents, an athlete choosing between a tournament and exams.
Every trial follows one layout. Section A: five 10-mark questions, usually split 2 + 4 + 4 or 3 + 3 + 4. Section B: two 10-mark questions, usually split 2 + 8. Section C: two 20-mark questions, usually split 4 + 8 + 8. The marks go to explaining, not naming: the largest share asks for actions, ways and roles, then effects and importance.

How the skema awards marks
  • 2 and 3 marks: one mark per point. The skema lists more points than needed and accepts any reasonable one ("terima mana-mana jawapan munasabah").
  • 4 marks: usually two points, each a fakta (F) plus a huraian (H) that explains it. Write the explanation, not just the fact.
  • 8 marks: four F + H points. 19 of the 84 8-mark parts are marked by level instead (Aras 1–4, 7–8 marks for Aras 4). A strong point gives the fact, explains it, adds an example and ends with its result.
  • Section C: 27 of the 56 8-mark parts, in 31 of the 34 Section C questions, say "berdasarkan nilai universal": name the value (for example Rasional, Bertanggungjawab, Kejujuran, Hormat, Kerjasama, Patriotisme) and tie each point to it.
  • Openers: "Apakah perasaan anda" wants a feeling plus the reason. "Wajarkah ... Justifikasikan" wants a clear stand first ("Wajar" / "Tidak wajar"), then reasons.

How this report was built
  1. Sources. 17 state trial papers of Pendidikan Moral (1225/1) from the drill: 10 from 2025 (Kedah, Kelantan, Labuan, Melaka, Pahang, Perak, Perlis, Sabah, Sarawak, Terengganu) and 7 from 2026 (Kelantan, Melaka 1 and 2, Pahang, Perlis, Sarawak, Selangor 1), 153 questions with their official skema.
  2. One paper counted once. The drill holds the Pahang 2026 paper three times (also as Pahang Set 1 and Set 2 "2025"; the questions are identical and one news cutting is dated April 2026). It is counted once here.
  3. Tagging. Each question was tagged by hand to the KSSM unit it tests most (12 Form 4 and 12 Form 5 units) and to a real-world issue. Each part was sorted by what it asks (feelings, stance, causes, effects, importance, actions, characteristics) from its wording.
  4. Trends. A theme's strength is the number of papers that set it. 2025 and 2026 are compared as shares of papers, since there are 10 and 7.

These are trial papers only. There are no actual SPM Pendidikan Moral papers in this set to check the trials against, and trial setters copy each other, so a popular issue is not certain to come. Use this to decide where extra practice goes, and still cover all 24 units.
