The real SPM papers in the drill (Kertas 1: 2023, 2024, Ulangan 2024; Kertas 2: 2022–2025 and Ulangan 2024) tagged question by question and compared with 26 Kertas 1 and 11 Kertas 2 state trials from 2025 and 2026.

Short answer: Kertas 2 works from a fixed menu. Four question types came in all five real papers (2022, 2023, 2024, Ulangan 2024, 2025): linear programming, matrices to solve simultaneous equations, logic statements and a financial plan or cash flow, and every Section B has a statistics question. Section B also seems to run on a two-year cycle: 2022 and 2024 share Venn diagrams, linear programming and income tax, while 2023 and 2025 share networks, matrices, financial planning and frequency graphs. If that holds, Section B in 2026 looks like 2024. Kertas 1 re-asks old ideas: 19 of SPM 2024's 40 questions repeated an idea from SPM 2023. The trials spend time on things the real Kertas 1 has not set, such as plans and elevations (10 of 17 trials of 2026, never in a real paper).

In all five real papers, as a full Section B question in 2022 and 2024 (Q14 both years) and inside Section C in Ulangan 2024 and 2025. 8 of 10 trials of 2025 and MRSM 2026 set it. Practise turning a word problem into inequalities, drawing the lines and reading the maximum or minimum.

How this report was built
  1. Sources. The real SPM Matematik papers in the drill: Kertas 1 for 2023, 2024 and Ulangan 2024 (SPM 2022 and 2025 Kertas 1 are not available), and Kertas 2 for 2022, 2023, 2024, Ulangan 2024 and 2025. State trials: Kertas 1 from 9 states in 2025 and 17 in 2026; Kertas 2 from 10 states in 2025 and MRSM in 2026.
  2. Tagging. Every Kertas 1 question and every Kertas 2 part was coded to one of 53 KSSM chapters of Forms 1–5, a question type, and a one-line description of the task: 1159 Kertas 1 questions and 272 Kertas 2 questions.
  3. Idea families. Kertas 1 descriptions were grouped into 171 families of the same idea across all papers, and Kertas 2 questions into 40 question types. A re-ask is a real-paper question whose family appeared in an earlier real paper.
  4. Forecast. Kertas 2 predictions follow what the board has set every year and which way the Section B cycle points, then are checked against the trials. Kertas 1 predictions rest on chapters and ideas set in more than one real paper.

Only three real Kertas 1 papers and five real Kertas 2 papers are available, and a few questions in them could not be read fully (some figures in SPM 2023 Kertas 1, and parts of the SPM 2022 Kertas 2 answers). The board can break any pattern. Use this to decide where extra practice goes, not to skip chapters.
