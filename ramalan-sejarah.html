Q1–3 — ALAM_MELAYU / Melaka polity
Every paper opens with Q1–3 covering Kesultanan Melayu Melaka: Sistem Empat Lipatan (Bendahara, Temenggung, Syahbandar, Laksamana), concept of daulat, undang-undang adat (Hukum Kanun Melaka, Undang-99 Perak), Raja-Rakyat relationship (timbal balik/kesetiaan). SPM 2025 confirms: Q1 Sistem Empat Lipatan, Q2 peranan Sultan/rakyat. These opening questions are near-scripted.
Q3–6 — KUASA_BARAT block (Barat/Jepun)
SPM 2023/2024 and SPMU papers all place Western powers and WWII topics here. SPM 2025 confirms this strongly: Q3 pemerintahan beraja Barat, Q4 Revolusi Amerika, Q5 Perjanjian Versailles/Jerman, Q6 dasar Jepun di Tanah Melayu. Expect Q3–6 to be KUASA_BARAT in SPM 2026.
Q23–Q28 — PERLEMBAGAAN block (F5)
Every paper allocates Q22–28 to constitutional topics. Charter of Justice (menghalang penindasan) appeared in SPM 2023 Q24 AND SPM 2024 Q23 AND SPM 2025 Q24. Demokrasi Berparlimen appeared in SPM 2023 Q25, SPM 2024 Q25, AND SPM 2025 Q25 — three consecutive actual SPM papers at Q25. The single strongest prediction in this entire analysis.
Q29–Q33 — PEMBENTUKAN MALAYSIA block
Every paper allocates questions here to the formation of Malaysia: Tunku's proposal, 1963 perlembagaan amendment, delay from Aug 31 to Sep 16 (konfrontasi / UN committee), MA63 intipati for Sabah/Sarawak. SPM 2025 allocated 5 questions (Q29–Q33) to this block — the most of any paper.
Q38–Q40 — DASAR_LUAR block
SPM 2025 confirms a strong DASAR_LUAR block at the end of F5: Q38 Apartheid/CHOGM, Q39 pendirian dadah di PBB, Q40 cadangan Antartika PBB sesi ke-37. This block also confirmed in SPM 2023/2024/SPMU papers. Expect Q36–40 to cover PEMBANGUNAN + DASAR_LUAR in SPM 2026.

Method used
  1. Map every question. All 28 Kertas 2 papers (7 real, 21 trials) were tagged by KSSM bab and by theme. The tags are in the Drill app's Topic Map.
  2. Treat the real papers as the evidence. Only SPM and SPMU papers count toward the forecast. Trials are a second view of what teachers expect, not evidence of what the board sets.
  3. Check the Ulangan effect before using it. Each SPMU paper was compared with the November paper of the same year, against a baseline of normal year-to-year overlap (Test 1). The effect was not consistent, so SPMU 2026 was not used to remove topics.
  4. Forecast by slot, then by angle. The paper keeps a stable shape (which part of the syllabus each Q number draws from). Within each likely bab, the forecast lists the angles the board has not used yet. It does not bet on a topic being "due", because that idea has repeatedly failed (Darurat: 0 of 7).
  5. Give confidence in tiers. "Certain bab" means the bab appears in nearly every paper. The exact question never is certain.

Limits: 7 real papers is a small sample, and the Ulangan test rests on only two years. Bab tags were assigned by reading each question and could be argued in a few edge cases. Analysis method

Each question was tagged with its topic cluster and key concept. Predictions are assigned confidence tiers based on:

  • Certain — topic appears in 4–5 papers, or the same concept appeared verbatim across 2+ actual SPM papers in consecutive years.
  • High — topic appears in 2–3 papers with consistent angle; or appears in both 2026 trial papers.
  • Moderate — topic appears in 1–2 papers; core syllabus but less consistent cross-paper evidence.

Sejarah predictions are based on 3 actual SPM papers (2023/2024/2025) + 2 SPMU papers (2023/2025) = 5 papers and 200 questions. The confidence tiers rest on actual examination papers. State trials are used to choose angles within a bab and, since the 2025 check, as supporting evidence where they agree with the real paper's slot history.
